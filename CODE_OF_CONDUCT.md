# Code of conduct

Coffee Sentinel is a small project maintained by volunteers. We expect
everyone who participates (issues, pull requests, discussions) to:

* Be kind and assume good intent. People have different levels of
  experience with C++, electronics and English.
* Keep feedback about the work, not the person. "This loop can block the
  buzzer" is useful; "this is sloppy" is not.
* Respect decisions once they are made and documented (see the ADRs). Reopen
  them with new arguments, not by relitigating.
* Give credit, and do not submit work that is not yours to license under MIT.

Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind are not
tolerated. Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content and block
participants who break these rules.

To report a problem, open an issue or e-mail the maintainers listed on the
repository page. Reports are handled discreetly.

This document is intentionally short. If it ever proves insufficient, the
project will adopt the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
